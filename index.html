The Horace Rumpole Project

Episode commentary for Rumpole of the Bailey

Pilot 1975

  • S00E01
    Rumpole and the Confession of Guilt Tuesday, 16 December 1975

    A barrister defends a Jamaican-British teenager accused of a seemingly motiveless stabbing near Lord's, in the standalone 1975 production that serves as the series' de facto pilot.

Series 1 1978

  • S01E01
    Rumpole and the Younger Generation Monday, 3 April 1978

    1967 case: Rumpole defends a Timson clan member; his own son seems to pick up Timson traits.

  • S01E02
    Rumpole and the Alternative Society Monday, 10 April 1978

    1970 case: hippie commune schoolteacher on a cannabis charge; an entrapment defence; Rumpole is drawn to her lifestyle.

  • S01E03
    Rumpole and the Honourable Member Monday, 17 April 1978

    1974 case: an MP on a rape charge; his self-incriminating testimony leads to conviction; Nick and Erica move to Baltimore.

  • S01E04
    Rumpole and the Married Lady Monday, 24 April 1978

    1975 divorce case; overheard calls lead Hilda to leave for Dodo's; opposing counsel is George Frobisher.

  • S01E05
    Rumpole and the Learned Friends Monday, 1 May 1978

    Rumpole accuses a dishonest policeman of framing his safecracker client, and gets into legal trouble when he cannot back the claim.

  • S01E06
    Rumpole and the Heavy Brigade

    Rumpole defends a stuttering petty crook on a murder charge.

Series 2 1979

  • S02E01
    Rumpole and the Man of God Tuesday, 29 May 1979

    A vicar is accused of shoplifting three shirts; the client refuses to testify on his own behalf.

  • S02E02
    Rumpole and the Case of Identity Tuesday, 5 June 1979

    Dave Anstey is charged with liquor-store robbery and GBH; a mistaken-identity defence; Anstey was framed by his employer.

  • S02E03
    Rumpole and the Show Folk Tuesday, 12 June 1979

    Actress Maggie Hartley is charged with murdering her husband; Rumpole wins an accidental-discharge acquittal, then learns she withheld money owed to him.

  • S02E04
    Rumpole and the Fascist Beast Tuesday, 19 June 1979

    A retired captain is charged under the Race Relations Act; Rumpole has taken on a Punjabi pupil barrister.

  • S02E05
    Rumpole and the Course of True Love Tuesday, 26 June 1979

    An English teacher is found guilty of seducing a pupil; Phyllida's pregnancy and marriage to Erskine-Brown; Featherstone's judgeship pressure.

  • S02E06
    Rumpole and the Age for Retirement Tuesday, 3 July 1979

    Elderly Percy Timson is accused of receiving stolen property; his family set up the crime to force his retirement -- and pressure Rumpole to retire too.

Specials 1980

  • S02E07
    Rumpole's Return Tuesday, 30 December 1980

    The 1980 TV special, not a true Series 2 episode: a bored, retired Rumpole living in Florida returns to London chambers after an inquiry from Phyllida draws him back.

Series 3 1983

  • S03E01
    Rumpole and the Genuine Article Tuesday, 11 October 1983

    An art dealer is accused of selling a forged painting; Rumpole shows it's genuine and the defendant tried to frame himself.

  • S03E02
    Rumpole and the Golden Thread Tuesday, 18 October 1983

    Rumpole defends a government minister accused of murdering a bishop in the African state of Neranga; acquittal, then his political ruin.

  • S03E03
    Rumpole and the Old Boy Net Tuesday, 25 October 1983

    A couple is accused of blackmailing a senior civil servant who was secretly an investor in their brothel; Rumpole loses the Head of Chambers post to Sam Ballard.

  • S03E04
    Rumpole and the Female of the Species Tuesday, 1 November 1983

    Anthony Timson is accused of bank robbery; Phyllida takes silk and Rumpole becomes her junior.

  • S03E05
    Rumpole and the Sporting Life Tuesday, 8 November 1983

    A woman says she accidentally shot her husband; a second bullet suggests a second shooter; she is acquitted after revealing an affair.

  • S03E06
    Rumpole and the Last Resort Tuesday, 15 November 1983

    A mobile-home park owner is charged with fraud; Rumpole fakes his own death to smoke out a former solicitor.

Series 4 1987

  • S04E01
    Rumpole and the Old, Old Story Monday, 19 January 1987

    A man is accused of attempted murder after brake sabotage; Rumpole moves into chambers after a row with Hilda.

  • S04E02
    Rumpole and the Blind Tasting Monday, 26 January 1987

    A Timson is rearrested over a stolen case of wine; Rumpole is knocked out early in a blind wine tasting.

  • S04E03
    Rumpole and the Official Secret Monday, 2 February 1987

    A civil servant is prosecuted under the Official Secrets Act; a colleague confesses to staging the leak and takes his own life.

  • S04E04
    Rumpole and the Judge's Elbow Monday, 9 February 1987

    A doctor is accused of running a disorderly massage parlour; Judge Featherstone's secret visit complicates the case.

  • S04E05
    Rumpole and the Bright Seraphim Monday, 16 February 1987

    A soldier is accused of murdering his abusive sergeant in Germany; Rumpole shows the timeline is wrong.

  • S04E06
    Rumpole's Last Case Wednesday, 25 February 1987

    Rumpole, defending a Timson, places an exotic racing bet that could let him retire if it wins.

Series 5 1988

  • S05E01
    Rumpole and the Bubble Reputation Wednesday, 23 November 1988

    A tabloid hires Rumpole to defend it in a libel suit brought by a puritanical novelist it accused of promiscuity.

  • S05E02
    Rumpole and the Barrow Boy Wednesday, 30 November 1988

    Nigel Timson, one of the few legitimate Timsons, is defended on an insider-trading charge.

  • S05E03
    Rumpole and the Age of Miracles Wednesday, 7 December 1988

    Hilda's nephew, a canon accused of adultery, is defended in an ecclesiastical court.

  • S05E04
    Rumpole and the Tap End Wednesday, 14 December 1988

    Featherstone's controversial remarks and ruling in a domestic violence case cause women's-rights fallout and Lord Chancellor concern.

  • S05E05
    Rumpole and Portia Wednesday, 21 December 1988

    A scrap dealer is accused of selling arms to terrorists; Phyllida presides as a newly appointed recorder.

  • S05E06
    Rumpole and the Quality of Life Wednesday, 28 December 1988

    A noblewoman is charged with murdering her elderly husband; subplots on discrimination at Equity Court and Rumpole's diet.

Series 6 1991

  • S06E01
    Rumpole à la Carte Monday, 28 October 1991

    Rumpole defends an elitist restaurateur he dislikes, after a live mouse jumps out of a gourmet meal.

  • S06E02
    Rumpole and the Summer of Discontent Monday, 4 November 1991

    A union activist is accused of manslaughter; Hilda goes on strike as cook over Rumpole's late hours.

  • S06E03
    Rumpole and the Right to Silence Monday, 11 November 1991

    A radical professor accused of murder refuses to disclose his alibi; Ballard and Erskine-Brown learn domestic lessons on the right to silence.

  • S06E04
    Rumpole at Sea Monday, 18 November 1991

    On a Mediterranean cruise Rumpole meets his old nemesis Judge Graves, and a fellow passenger disappears unexplained.

  • S06E05
    Rumpole and the Quacks Monday, 25 November 1991

    A doctor is accused of molesting a patient; Phyllida suspects Erskine-Brown of philandering.

  • S06E06
    Rumpole for the Prosecution Monday, 2 December 1991

    Rumpole prosecutes for the first time, in a private complaint from the rich father of a murdered girl.

Series 7 1992

  • S07E01
    Rumpole and the Children of the Devil Thursday, 29 October 1992

    A well-meaning social worker takes custody of a young Timson girl after claims of devil worship in the family.

  • S07E02
    Rumpole and the Miscarriage of Justice Thursday, 5 November 1992

    Rumpole defends a police officer charged with falsifying a confession.

  • S07E03
    Rumpole and the Eternal Triangle Thursday, 12 November 1992

    Rumpole becomes infatuated with a violinist and ends up defending her husband on a charge of murdering her lover.

  • S07E04
    Rumpole and the Reform of Joby Jonson Thursday, 19 November 1992

    A juvenile delinquent is accused of assaulting an elderly woman; Hilda is worried when a burglar tries to steal evidence from Rumpole's briefcase.

  • S07E05
    Rumpole and the Family Pride Thursday, 26 November 1992

    A suspicious drowning on a lord's estate; he invites Rumpole and Hilda for a weekend and asks Horace to represent him at the inquest.

  • S07E06
    Rumpole on Trial Thursday, 3 December 1992

    Rumpole is hauled before a bar disciplinary tribunal for allegedly coaching a client mid-testimony and insulting the judge -- but the true target of his overheard outburst turns out to be someone else entirely.
