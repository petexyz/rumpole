The Oxford Book of English Verse, 1250–1900

Joshua Sylvester 1563-1618

115. Ubique

Were I as base as is the lowly plain, And you, my Love, as high as heaven above, Yet should the thoughts of me, your humble swain, Ascend to heaven in honour of my love. Were I as high as heaven above the plain, And you, my Love, as humble and as low As are the deepest bottoms of the main, Wheresoe’er you were, with you my love should go. Were you the earth, dear Love, and I the skies, My love should shine on you like to the Sun, And look upon you with ten thousand eyes, Till heaven wax’d blind, and till the world were done. Wheresoe’er I am,--below, or else above you-- Wheresoe’er you are, my heart shall truly love you.
