The Oxford Book of English Verse, 1250–1900

Margaret L. Woods b. 1856

882. Genius Loci

Peace, Shepherd, peace! What boots it singing on? Since long ago grace-giving Phœbus died, And all the train that loved the stream-bright side Of the poetic mount with him are gone Beyond the shores of Styx and Acheron, In unexplorèd realms of night to hide. The clouds that strew their shadows far and wide Are all of Heaven that visits Helicon. Yet here, where never muse or god did haunt, Still may some nameless power of Nature stray, Pleased with the reedy stream’s continual chant And purple pomp of these broad fields in May. The shepherds meet him where he herds the kine, And careless pass him by whose is the gift divine.
