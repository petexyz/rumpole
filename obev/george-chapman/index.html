George Chapman 1560-1634

107. Bridal Song

O come, soft rest of cares! come, Night! Come, naked Virtue’s only tire, The reapèd harvest of the light Bound up in sheaves of sacred fire. Love calls to war: Sighs his alarms, Lips his swords are, The field his arms.
Come, Night, and lay thy velvet hand On glorious Day’s outfacing face; And all thy crownèd flames command For torches to our nuptial grace. Love calls to war: Sighs his alarms, Lips his swords are, The field his arms.

Source: Project Gutenberg ebook #66619, The Oxford Book of English Verse, 1250–1900, ed. Arthur Quiller-Couch (1931 impression). Public domain in the US.
