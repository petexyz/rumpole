The Oxford Book of English Verse, 1250–1900

John Boyle O’Reilly 1844-1890

831. A White Rose

The red rose whispers of passion, And the white rose breathes of love; O, the red rose is a falcon, And the white rose is a dove.
But I send you a cream-white rosebud With a flush on its petal tips; For the love that is purest and sweetest Has a kiss of desire on the lips.

Source: Project Gutenberg ebook #66619, The Oxford Book of English Verse, 1250–1900, ed. Arthur Quiller-Couch (1931 impression). Public domain in the US.
