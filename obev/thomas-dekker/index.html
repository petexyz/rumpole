Thomas Dekker 1575-1641

204. Sweet Content

Art thou poor, yet hast thou golden slumbers? O sweet content! Art thou rich, yet is thy mind perplex’d? O punishment! Dost thou laugh to see how fools are vex’d To add to golden numbers golden numbers? O sweet content! O sweet, O sweet content! Work apace, apace, apace, apace; Honest labour bears a lovely face; Then hey nonny nonny--hey nonny nonny!
Canst drink the waters of the crispèd spring? O sweet content! Swim’st thou in wealth, yet sink’st in thine own tears? O punishment! Then he that patiently want’s burden bears, No burden bears, but is a king, a king! O sweet content! O sweet, O sweet content! Work apace, apace, apace, apace; Honest labour bears a lovely face; Then hey nonny nonny--hey nonny nonny!
