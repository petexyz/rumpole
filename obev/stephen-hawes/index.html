Stephen Hawes d. 1523

32. The True Knight

For knighthood is not in the feats of warre, As for to fight in quarrel right or wrong, But in a cause which truth can not defarre: He ought himself for to make sure and strong, Justice to keep mixt with mercý among: And no quarrell a knight ought to take But for a truth, or for the common’s sake.

32. defarre] undo.

33. An Epitaph

O mortal folk, you may behold and see How I lie here, sometime a mighty knight; The end of joy and all prosperitee Is death at last, thorough his course and might: After the day there cometh the dark night, For though the daye be never so long, At last the bells ringeth to evensong.
