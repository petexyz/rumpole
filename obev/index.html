The Oxford Book of English Verse

1250–1900 · chosen & edited by Arthur Quiller-Couch

This is a complete HTML edition of The Oxford Book of English Verse (1900; this text from the 1931 impression), Arthur Quiller-Couch's anthology of English verse from the thirteenth century to the close of the nineteenth. It is Project Gutenberg ebook #66619, confirmed public domain in the United States, and is reproduced here from that text, poet by poet, in the book's own order and with its own sequential poem numbering (1 through 884).

It belongs on this site because Rumpole (the barrister this project is otherwise about) names this exact anthology outright in his "golden thread" closing speech in S03E02, "Rumpole and the Golden Thread": that England will be remembered for three things, "the British breakfast, the Oxford Book of English Verse, and the presumption of innocence." A secondary source ties him specifically to this 1900 Quiller-Couch edition, not its 1972 successor. See this project's own Project Gutenberg source for the original ebook, and ideas/literary-quotes/ (not published here) for this project's wider catalog of Rumpole's on-screen quotations, many of them traced to poems in this very book.
