About this site

An unofficial reference to Rumpole of the Bailey

Created by Simon Valvo (simon.valvo@proton.me).

This is a fan-made reference to Rumpole of the Bailey, John Mortimer's Old Bailey barrister, in the television series and in the books. The television run is 44 programmes: a 1975 BBC Play for Today that served as the pilot, Series 1 to 7 made by Thames Television for ITV between 1978 and 1992, and the 1980 special Rumpole's Return. Leo McKern played Horace Rumpole throughout.

What is here

Each episode has its own page: a plot summary in this site's own words, the date and channel of its first UK showing, and a credits block giving the director, the writer and the cast with the roles they played. The Characters page follows the regulars and the actors who played them, including the recasts, and the Books page lists the short story collections, novels and omnibus volumes with their UK and US titles.

Episodes are numbered by series and episode in order of first showing, so S03E02 is the second programme of Series 3. The 1975 pilot is S00E01. Rumpole's Return sits between Series 2 and Series 3, where it was actually shown.

The name

Rumpolian is the fan's word for a follower of Rumpole, in the way Sherlockian is for Holmes. The spelling varies in print (Rumpolean turns up as well); this site uses Rumpolian.

What this site is not

It has no connection with John Mortimer's estate, Thames Television, ITV, the BBC or any publisher. The programmes and books are still in copyright, so the site carries no scripts, no extended quotations, no audio and no stills. The plot summaries are written from watching the programmes and are meant to help you find an episode or recall one, not to stand in for watching it.

How far to trust it

Air dates and channels come from published broadcast records. The cast and crew lists are compiled from published credit listings and may still contain gaps or mistakes, especially for the early episodes and for small parts. Transmission times are not listed because no reliable source for them has turned up. Corrections are welcome.
