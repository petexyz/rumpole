Known discrepancies on the source page itself

Wikipedia's own body text and its navigation box/collections section disagree on three dates:

  • Rumpole and the Angel of Death: 1994 in the body, 1995 elsewhere on the same page.
  • Rumpole Rests His Case: 2001 in the body, 2002 in the navbox.
  • Rumpole and the Primrose Path: 2002 in the body, 2003 in the navbox.

The page's navbox also lists a "Rumpole of the Bailey (1980)" entry that appears to be a mislabeled link to Rumpole's Return, not a separate book.

These weren't resolved against a second source; treat the dates above as "per Wikipedia," not as independently verified.
